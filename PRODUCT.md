# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mark — a single individual in the Philippines tracking his own money. Paid
semi-monthly (15th & 30th). Uses the app on his **phone** most of the time
(evenings, quick checks after a purchase or on payday) and on a laptop
occasionally for a fuller look. Not an accountant — he wants clarity and
confidence, not a spreadsheet. This is a private, one-person app; every screen
serves an audience of one.

## Product Purpose

A personal finance tracker that replaces a Google Sheet. It answers, at a
glance: *what did I actually take home, where is it going, who owes whom, and
am I saving?* Six jobs:

- **Salary** — enter gross for a cutoff, get an accurate **net** via 2026 PH
  statutory math (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR semi-monthly withholding).
- **Expenses** — fast capture, category totals, top merchants.
- **Loans (utang)** — money Mark owes; payoff progress. Seeded with a ₱25k
  emergency loan.
- **Pautang** — money owed *to* Mark; who, how much, paid/partial/unpaid.
- **Savings** — running balance against a goal.
- **Dashboard + Calendar** — monthly summary, charts, purchases-on-a-day,
  habit tracking.

Success = Mark opens it instead of the sheet, trusts the net-pay number, and
can see his savings rate for the month without doing any math himself.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, editorial, precise.** Reads like a well-set personal magazine about
*his* money, not a corporate fintech dashboard. Confident typography does the
talking; color is spent deliberately. Calm and legible first; character in the
headlines and the one warm accent. Voice is plain and human ("You took home
₱X this cutoff"), never jargon.

## Anti-references

- **DVAC's "Red Signal"** — that's a different project and owns bright
  signal-red. This app must not look like it.
- **The warm-cream / beige AI-dashboard cliché** — warmth here comes from the
  accent and the type, on a clean (near-white or deep) surface. No parchment
  backgrounds.
- **Neon fintech / crypto-bro dashboards** — glowing gradients, hero-metric
  gradient text, casino energy. No.
- **A spreadsheet** — dense grids of undifferentiated cells with no hierarchy.
- **Generic identical-card SaaS grids** — icon + heading + text repeated.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers are the hero.** Money is the most important content on every
   screen — tabular, aligned, unambiguous. Hierarchy from size and weight, not
   decoration.
2. **Warmth from type and one accent, never the background.** The editorial
   feel lives in the display face and a single warm accent, spent where it
   carries meaning (primary action, the current thing).
3. **Phone-first, thumb-reachable.** Primary actions sit where a thumb lands;
   entry is fast; targets are generous.
4. **One screen, one job.** Each view answers a single question; the dashboard
   summarizes, the detail views capture.
5. **Calm by default, color only where it means something.** Red for money
   going out / debt, positive for savings — used sparingly, not everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, placeholders held
to body contrast. Full keyboard paths and visible focus. Tap targets ≥ 44px.
Charts never rely on color alone (labels + values present). Honor
`prefers-reduced-motion` with reduced/instant alternatives. Currency always
formatted `₱#,##0.00`.
